# Statement of Work — Invoice Export Platform

**Client:** Larkspur Labs Ltd. (billing@larkspur.example)
**Contractor:** [Your Name], independent software developer
**Effective date:** 12 October 2026
**Currency:** USD

## 1. Scope
Contractor will build a web application that lets Larkspur's finance team manage
customer accounts and export invoices. Work is delivered to the public repository
`[your-github]/larkspur-invoice-export`.

## 2. Milestones and fees (fixed price)
| # | Milestone | Acceptance criteria | Fee | Due |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Authentication module | Email/password signup, login, password reset; tests passing | $600 | 24 Oct 2026 |
| 2 | Invoice export API | CSV and PDF export endpoints for invoices; depends on Milestone 1 | $900 | 7 Nov 2026 |
| 3 | Admin dashboard | Admin can view, filter and download exports; depends on Milestone 2 | $700 | 21 Nov 2026 |

## 3. Change requests
Work outside Section 2 is billed hourly at **$40/hour**, capped at **20 hours**
unless approved in writing. Total contract value shall not exceed **$3,000**.

## 4. Payment terms
Each milestone is invoiced on acceptance. Invoices are payable **Net 15** via PayPal.
Partial payments are accepted; the minimum payment is 25% of the invoice.
Overdue balances accrue a late fee of **1.5% per month**.

## 5. Acceptance
Client has 5 business days after a milestone is marked complete to accept or
reject it with written reasons. Silence after 5 business days counts as acceptance.

## 6. Termination
Either party may terminate with 7 days' notice. Completed and accepted milestones
remain payable.
